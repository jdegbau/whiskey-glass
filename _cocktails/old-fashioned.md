ingredients:
  - 2 oz High-Rye Bourbon
  - 1/4 oz Demerara Syrup
  - 2 dashes Black Walnut Bitters
  - 1 dash Orange Bitters
  - Large clear ice cube
  - Wide orange twist (for garnish)
---

This sophisticated riff on the classic Old Fashioned uses dark, earthy walnut bitters to complement the caramel notes of a high-rye bourbon. It is the signature drink for the **whiskey.glass** aesthetic.

### Instructions

1. **Chill the Glass:** Place a rocks glass in the freezer for 5 minutes or fill with ice water while you prepare the drink.
2. **Combine:** In a mixing glass, combine the bourbon, demerara syrup, and both types of bitters.
3. **Stir:** Add plenty of ice to the mixing glass and stir gently with a long bar spoon for about 30 seconds. This chills the drink and achieves the perfect dilution.
4. **Strain:** Empty your chilled rocks glass. Place one large clear ice cube inside and strain the mixture over the ice.
5. **Garnish:** Express the oils of an orange twist over the surface of the drink, rub the rim of the glass with the peel, and drop it in.

### Why it Works
The high-rye content provides a spicy backbone that prevents the demerara sugar from feeling overly cloying, while the walnut bitters add a savory depth that lingers on the finish.
